How it works

  1. Open a page with a form and fill it in the way you like.
  2. Click the Formwise icon, then Save this form.
  3. Next time, click the icon (or press Alt+Shift+F) and the page fills itself in.

Questions

Is my form data uploaded anywhere?

No. Saved forms are kept in your browser's local extension storage and never leave your computer. The only outside service involved is our payment provider (ExtensionPay), which is contacted to check whether Pro is unlocked. Your forms are never part of that.

Does it save passwords or card numbers?

No. Password fields and credit card fields are skipped on purpose, so they are never saved or filled.

Why doesn't Formwise ask for access to every website?

Filling from the toolbar icon or the shortcut only touches the page you are on, at the moment you ask. Only if you turn on "fill automatically when the page opens" (Pro) does Chrome ask you to allow that one site.

I'm coming from Lightning Autofill. Can I keep my forms?

Yes. Export your data from Lightning Autofill as a .csv file, then use Import on Formwise's saved-forms page.

I paid for Pro on another computer. How do I unlock it here?

Open Formwise's saved-forms page, click Already paid? and enter the email you paid with.

Something isn't working
